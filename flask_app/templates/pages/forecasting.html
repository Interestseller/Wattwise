How Forecasting Works

Recursive Multi-Step Forecasting

The model was trained to predict one step ahead. For longer horizons, each prediction is fed back as a lag feature for the next step.

  • Calendar features capture daily & seasonal patterns
  • Lag features encode short-term autocorrelation
  • Rolling statistics capture recent volatility
  • Uncertainty grows with forecast horizon (√h scaling)

⚠ Uncertainty intervals are heuristic estimates, not statistically rigorous prediction intervals.
